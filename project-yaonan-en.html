NIGHT OF DEMON MAN

Chinese folklore reimagined — exploring the subtleties of human nature through Eastern mysticism.

Introduction

NIGHT OF DEMON MAN is an immersive performance in development by Surprise Night. Inspired by ancient Chinese folklore, this production combines Eastern mystic aesthetics with contemporary immersive theatre language to construct a magical world that blurs the line between reality and illusion.

Audiences will enter a mysterious nocturnal world, encountering various spirit characters and exploring the subtleties of human nature within interwoven spaces of reality and fantasy. The work seeks to reinterpret traditional folklore culture through a contemporary lens, allowing ancient Eastern imagination to find new life through immersive experience.

Creative Concept

Chinese folklore has a long and rich history, from the Classic of Mountains and Seas to Strange Tales from a Chinese Studio, accumulating abundant resources of imagination. NIGHT OF DEMON MAN draws inspiration from these traditional texts, reinterpreting them through contemporary theatrical language to create a mysterious world that is both familiar and strange.

The work explores the boundary between the "other" and the "normal," examining human nature through the perspective of spirit characters and exploring eternal themes of identity, desire, and taboo within an atmosphere of mystery.

Experience Highlights

  • Eastern Folklore Aesthetic — Visual and narrative inspiration drawn from traditional folklore texts, creating a unique atmosphere of Eastern mysticism.
  • Nocturnal Immersive Experience — Set against the backdrop of night, utilizing multi-sensory elements including light, sound, and scent to construct a mysterious world.
  • Spirit Character Interaction — Close encounters with various spirit characters, each with unique stories and personalities.
  • Multi-threaded Narrative Structure — Audiences choose their own paths of exploration, triggering different story segments and endings.
  • Contemporary Tradition — Reinterpreting traditional folklore culture through a modern lens, allowing ancient stories to find new life.
  • Cross-genre Music Design — Blending traditional folk music with electronic music to create a unique auditory experience.
